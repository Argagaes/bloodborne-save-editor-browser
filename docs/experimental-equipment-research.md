# Experimental equipment research

The reference tables are the Bloodborne Wiki's Datamined Weapon Model & Item IDs and Datamined Armor Model & Item IDs. Only item-ID tables were used; model IDs are not inventory IDs. Wiki decimal item IDs were compared with the editor's normal weapon and armor catalogs and with row IDs from EquipParamWeapon and EquipParamProtector in the installed CUSA03173 1.09 gameparam bundle. The experimental list contains base weapon records (weapon upgrade rows are excluded) and armor rows that were found in the inspected parameters but not in the catalog. Display labels for roaming weapons are derived from the corresponding ordinary weapon name and the wiki's Japanese label; translations are approximate. Protector rows include appearance/body entries and other internal records, not necessarily ordinary clothing.

A parameter row is not evidence that a record can be added, rendered, equipped or used successfully. The editor uses its generic equipment record template; in-game behavior has not been tested. Reload/remove/undo were tested in editor fixtures. The lists are limited to the cited wiki pages, not an exhaustive survey of all installed parameters or DLC.

Sources: https://www.bloodborne-wiki.com/2018/01/datamined-weapon-model-item-ids.html and https://www.bloodborne-wiki.com/2018/01/datamined-armor-model-item-ids.html
