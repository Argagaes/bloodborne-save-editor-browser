# Blood gem builder research

The builder stores a 40-byte upgrade record with a shape and six effect IDs. Rating and the numbered grade shown by the editor come from the primary effect's catalog entry; they are not independently written as fields. The game constructs a cursed name from a gem with a negative effect. The catalog's primary names therefore usually omit the word “Cursed”: a Tempering Damp primary plus a negative effect can be displayed as Cursed Tempering Damp. Searching for “cursed” in the builder finds the corresponding primary and adds an editable stamina-cost effect.

A gem's second word is its GemGenParam source, also stored in the inventory entry. The bundled `gem-sources.json` maps 116 primary effect IDs to fixed-effect generation rows from the installed game with zero name offset; other effects use a standard non-unique source. Three special gems use their corresponding special source when their primary and shape match. This mapping does not establish that every effect/source combination produces the correct in-game name. Editing the primary or shape of a special gem changes its source to a standard source.

The builder allows radial, triangle, waning, circle, and droplet shapes, a primary effect filtered by rating 1–20, and five independently searchable additional effect slots. A rating 20 gem is Abyssal. Individual effect IDs and values are taken from the bundled upgrade catalog. Curse examples include stamina cost increases, attack reductions against beasts or kin, durability reduction, and HP drain. The builder does not enforce an enemy's effect pool, rank budget, or natural drop rules: available catalog effects and shape combinations include illegal or otherwise unverified results. Some secondary effects can have ratings different from the primary.

References: [gem terminology](https://www.bloodborne-wiki.com/2017/02/blood-gems-terminology.html), [how gems are determined](https://www.bloodborne-wiki.com/2017/04/how-are-blood-gems-determined.html), [datamined effect IDs](https://www.bloodborne-wiki.com/2018/01/datamined-blood-gem-effects.html), [secondary effects](https://www.bloodborne-wiki.com/2015/11/blood-gems-secondary-stats.html), [example gem setups](https://www.bloodborne-wiki.com/2015/10/best-blood-gems-setups.html), and [save layout and generation source](https://github.com/bloodborne-native/bloodborne-launcher/blob/main/docs/SAVE_EDITOR.md). The requested Fextralife pages were inaccessible to the research tool.

Creating a record moves later data by 32 bytes in the fixed-size save and drops the final 32 bytes, following the documented fixed-size approach. The editor can read the resulting record, but game acceptance and name rendering are not yet verified for arbitrary combinations. Keep a backup and test on a disposable copy.

The builder labels exact Red, Tear, and Gold special-gem records as “Verified” against their fixed GemGenParam rows (not in-game testing). Changes to their fixed effects are marked “Unobtainable from this source,” rather than being ruled out from every possible source. Other combinations are labeled “Potentially obtainable” as an explicit unverified fallback; the label does not establish a natural drop. No full pool validator has yet been implemented.
